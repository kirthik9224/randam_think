How to Play

1

Select Your Difficulty

Choose between Easy (1–50), Medium (1–100), Hard (1–500), or Expert (1–1000). Each round secretly generates a new random target number.

2

Submit Your Guess

Type your guess into the input field and press Enter or click Guess. You only have a limited number of attempts to find the secret number!

3

Follow the Clues

After each guess, the game gives you immediate direction:

  • ⬆️ Too Low — The secret number is higher.
  • ⬇️ Too High — The secret number is lower.
  • 🎉 Correct — You cracked the code and won!
4

Pro Strategy: Binary Search

To win efficiently, always guess the middle of the remaining range! For example, on 1–100, guess 50 first. If it's Too Low, your next ideal guess is 75!
